I'm Rajat, a first-year BCA student at Dr. Virendra Swarup Institute of Computer Studies, Kanpur. My journey started with C in the classroom and gradually expanded into web development, product thinking, and practical problem solving.

I started with the smallest possible steps — a hello world, then a calculator, then a program that checks whether someone is old enough to vote. From there I moved into full-stack work, small team projects, and application thinking that is closer to real software products.

I'm still early, and I'm okay with that. I'd rather build something small, break it, fix it and learn — than wait until I know everything. If you're building something and want an extra pair of hands, I'd love to be part of it.
