01 — About

Curious student,
hands-on builder.

I'm Rajat, a first-year BCA student at Dr. Virendra Swarup Institute of Computer Studies, Kanpur. My journey started with C in the classroom and quickly expanded into web projects, logic building, and real-world problem solving.

I started with the smallest possible steps — a hello world, then a calculator, then a program that checks whether someone is old enough to vote. From there I moved into full-stack work, building apps and interfaces that teach me both design and engineering.

I'm still early, and I'm okay with that. I'd rather build something small, break it, fix it and learn — than wait until I know everything. If you're building something and want an extra pair of hands, I'm always open to collaborating.
