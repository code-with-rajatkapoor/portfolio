01 — About

Curious student,
hands-on builder.

I'm Rajat, a first-year BCA student at Dr. Virendra Swarup Institute of Computer Studies, Kanpur. My journey started with C in the classroom and quickly turned into a habit of writing programs at home to see what I could make work.

I started with the smallest possible steps — a hello world, then a calculator, then a program that checks whether someone is old enough to vote. From there I joined Team Code Debuggers and we built a full-stack municipal corporation portal with role-based access for citizens and officers and real-time complaint registration, backed by Express.js and SQLite.

I'm still early, and I'm okay with that. I'd rather build something small, break it, fix it and learn — than wait until I know everything. If you're building something and want an extra pair of hands, I'd love to help.
